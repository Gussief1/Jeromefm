Heat Check

Brooce is a DJ and producer originally from the Maryland/Washington D.C. area, now based in Central Pennsylvania. His journey began as a producer, crafting experimental sounds and mixes for SoundCloud before gradually expanding his music to major streaming platforms. Over time, his passion led him to DJing, where he’s since brought his unique sound to venues across Pennsylvania and the DMV, including sets at Flash, Sinners & Saints, and appearances on stations like NTS Radio, Eaton Radio, and 91.7 WIXQ.

Raised in a home where the radio was always playing, Brooce absorbed a wide spectrum of music early on—from hip-hop and R&B to funk, soul, and rock. These eclectic influences continue to shape his musical identity, fueling a versatile taste that spans multiple genres. While House music is his main love, his sets often explore Drum & Bass, Downtempo, and Techno.

Brooce is also one-third of Nightcap, a DJ collective alongside Deniz and Collin—two highly talented selectors with distinct styles that complement and challenge each other. Together, they curate DIY events in Lancaster, PA, and previously held a residency at Little Mutants. Their sets are a blend of diverse sounds that, while different, always stay true to the collective’s ethos.
