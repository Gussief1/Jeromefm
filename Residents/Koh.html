Koh

Koh, short for the infamous koh-i-noor gemstone, has symbolically used this reference to develop their intersectional taste for bass music, South Asian folkloric sounds, and glitched-out techno. Koh's musical tastes and selections reflect the transcontinental and post-colonial experience of many South Asians over the past several hundred years. Koh is the programming director and owner of Transmission, a venue in DC pushing forward underground music. He is also one of the founders of the iconic DMV club-night series, Hast du Feuer.

A self-taught producer and DJ, Koh's first love of sound centred on the UK Dubstep and Bass movement of the 00s, influenced by labels like Deep Medi, Ninja Tune, and Hessle Audio. Feeling limited by the standards of western-oriented electronic music, they began to branch out in search of non-western instrumentation and rhythmic compositions. This journey has lead Koh to discover a new frontier of possibilities, combining elements of UK-inspired sounds, breaks and hard-driving techno tracks with Bhangra percussion and instruments like the Tabla.
