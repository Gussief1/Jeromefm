KUNAI

KUNAI is a ghost in the machine. A project built from the scars that never fully heal, where grief, memory, technology, and survival collide inside a world of broken networks, military hardware, surveillance systems, and fading human emotion. KUNAI draws from: UK Grime, Deconstructed Club, IDM, Trip-Hop, Hard Drum, Wave Music, Hard Vapor, and Experimental Ambient.
