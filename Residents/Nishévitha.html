Nishévitha

Nishévitha is a DJ, vocalist, curator, and emerging producer shaping the NJ/NYC electronic and club music landscape. Originally from the Bay Area and currently based in New Jersey by way of Atlanta, their genre-defying sets reflect a deep-rooted foundation in both South Indian and Western classical music—blending precision with emotional release.

Nishévitha has played at esteemed venues such as Mood Ring and Bossa Nova Civic Club, and the Lot Radio. Known for weaving together a meditative range of Vogue, Breaks, Footwork, Rap, Juke, Global Club, Gqom, and Techno, Nishévitha invites listeners to experience the dance floor as a resistant space for reform, transcendence, and healing.

As Co-founder of Club Hysteria, a NJ-based collective spotlighting global club sounds, she has curated vibrant events across the region, cementing her role as a tastemaker in the NYC/NJ scene. Her storytelling extends globally through her radio show Jaggery Radio—a show initially rooted in South Indian identity that has since evolved to celebrate Queer, Femme, and BIPOC artists from across the diaspora.
