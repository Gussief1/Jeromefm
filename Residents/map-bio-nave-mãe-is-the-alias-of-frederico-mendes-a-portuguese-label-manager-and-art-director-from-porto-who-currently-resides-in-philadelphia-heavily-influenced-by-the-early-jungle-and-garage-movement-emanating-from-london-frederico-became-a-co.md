bio: >-
  Nave Mãe is the alias of Frederico Mendes, a Portuguese Label Manager and Art
  Director from Porto who currently resides in Philadelphia. Heavily influenced
  by the early Jungle and Garage movement emanating from London, Frederico
  became a collector of world club scenes.
   
  The imprint he currently manages is 1980Lyfers, an independent record label with operations in Porto, South London, and the East Coast, US. 1980Lyfers has appeared in magazines like Wire and has debuted some of the first classic records from the most promising new producers.
   
  Additionally, Frederico keeps himself busy as the radio host of his own seasonal show at Radio AlHara and as a host at Eaton Radio, DC.
   
  He also co-founded an online platform called Them Local with Katarina Especial. They often promote artists, designers, and musicians from all over the world - providing support to help their ideas come to fruition.


  By exploring new places and different cultures, Nave Mãe’s performances reflect the diverging music scenes he’s been picked up along the way.
